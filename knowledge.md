## Automobile
Page: https://agc-assurances.com/assurance-auto/
- Compulsory motor third-party (Responsabilité Civile / RTI) under the CIMA code. Purpose: compensate traffic-accident victims and the solvency of the person at fault.
- Home page: 9 formulas for different needs.
- Guarantees listed: RC/RTI (compulsory); Défense & Recours; Individuelle personnes transportées / Individuel accident conducteur; Vol / vol partiel; Vol par braquage; Incendie; Bris de glaces et blocs feux; Tierce collision; Dommages tous accidents.
- Also offered according to needs: assistance à la réparation; assistance judiciaire; convention spéciale dommages aux premiers risques.
- Quote: https://agc-assurances.com/demande-de-devis/?produit=automobile
- Online auto quote form asks (among others): city of use, vehicle type, make, registration, grey-card scan, year licence obtained, year first registered, new value, market value, cover capital, then guarantees, then subscriber (branch, civility, name, email, address, phone, date of birth, NUI, profession, ID scan).
- FAQ — documents to subscribe motor: carte grise; certificat de vente; CIVIO.
- FAQ — vehicles: they insure all types of cars/vehicles except 4B (intercity/travel buses). Motorbikes, trucks etc. are not excluded in that FAQ sentence except 4B buses.
- FAQ — motor claim: (1) police/gendarmerie accident report (constat); (2) declare at any AGC Espace Clients or HQ; (3) claims department follows the file until indemnity according to the policy.
- How to subscribe (FAQ): visit an Espace Clients, call 694 133 876 / 694 577 813, email agc@agc-assurances.com, or use the online tariff tool linked from the auto page.

## Personal accident (Individuels accidents corporels)
On health page and AGC RCA:
- Pays indemnities in the special conditions if the insured suffers bodily injury from a guaranteed accident, at work or in private life.
- Accident = unintentional bodily injury from a sudden external cause. Illnesses/conditions consecutive to an accident are included.
- Can cover: medical/surgical/pharma/hospital costs; permanent disability (partial/total); accidental death; daily allowance; sanatorium/preventorium stay.
- “Le saviez-vous” on the multi-risk page: from 5 000 FCFA it is possible to be covered up to 250 000 FCFA for one year against everyday bodily accidents. Present as published on the site, not as a personalised price. Do not apply this figure to motor or health family plans.

## Travel (assurance voyage / assistance voyage)
Page: https://agc-assurances.com/assurance-voyage-2/
- Partner: Inter Partner Assistance (IPA), described as a world leader in medical assistance.
- Covers incidents before and during trips abroad.
- Guarantees listed on the product page: transport sanitaire / emergency medical assistance; Défense & Recours; emergency dental care; body repatriation; information if baggage delayed; advance of criminal bail and lawyer fees; early return to country of residence; hotel if convalescing; ticket for a relative to visit; hotel for that relative; early return if a relative dies; legal assistance abroad; transmission of urgent messages.
- RCA page also lists: sending medicines abroad; information if personal documents lost/stolen.
- Transport sanitaire (detail): if serious bodily injury in Cameroon, IPA’s medical team organises transfer to a more suitable hospital in Africa or elsewhere after agreement between the local doctor and IPA, if the person cannot be treated on the spot.
- Body repatriation (detail): IPA organises transport of the body or ashes to the nearest international airport to the burial place in Cameroon or the country named in the policy.

## Liability (responsabilité civile)
Pages: https://agc-assurances.com/responsabilite-civile/ and /responsabilite-civile-du-chef-entreprise/
- Home: pecuniary consequences of civil liability under articles 1382 to 1386 of the Civil Code.
- Chef de famille: solvency of the head of household for unintentional harm caused by them or people living under their roof.
- Professional RC is required for many liberal and building trades (medical, law, accountants, estate agents, insurance agents, communication, travel agents…).
- Types listed: Chef d’entreprise; Professionnelle; Chef de famille; Association; Sportive; Scolaire; Organisateurs d’évènements; Propriétaire immobilier; Coiffeur; Décennale.
- RCA extra types: floating craft (pleasure boat, fishing vessel); tenant (locative); hunter.
- If damage comes from the insured failing to perform a service, cover applies only if that failure is due to an event independent of the insured’s will (as published).
- Quote family RC: https://agc-assurances.com/demande-de-devis/?produit=responsabilite-civile-famille
